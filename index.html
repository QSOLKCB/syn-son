Preservation posture

Keep the archaeology; label the invention

The project links to the R.W. Gray web edition, institutional records, archived lecture material, and independent teaching pages. It does not republish the books or silently clean legacy web history into a new authority.

“Frequency” is namespace-sensitive. In the source it often means edge subdivision or shell count. Hertz enters only at the declared sonification layer. Likewise, 432 Hz is a chosen reference, not a Synergetics theorem.

The visual geometry is a schematic score display. Exact source mathematics, derived mappings, lore, and provisional relations remain separately labelled.

Independent work by Trent Slade / QSOL-IMC. Built to complement—not replace— primary texts, physical models, preserved web archaeology, and human teaching.
